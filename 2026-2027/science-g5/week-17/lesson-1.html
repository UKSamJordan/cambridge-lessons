📏 Steps to Success

  1. 1Draw a neat rectangle with a ruler (90° corners, zero gaps).
  2. 2Rub out clean slots for each component.
  3. 3Drop in official Cambridge symbols (keep the cell gap empty!).
  4. 4Check battery order: + to − in series.

⚠️ Classroom Safety Rules

  • ✅ Keep hands and tables completely dry.
  • ✅ Use low-voltage 1.5 V classroom cells only — NEVER touch wall mains sockets (220 V)!
  • ✅ Never connect a wire straight from + to − with no lamp/buzzer (short circuit gets hot!).
  • ✅ Disconnect immediately if any wire feels warm.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Prompts & Script

1. EAL Hinge Check (Cell vs Battery)

Hold up 1 AA cell: "One unit = cell (1.5 V). Two or more joined + to − = battery (3 V)." Check pupils leave a clean empty gap between the tall thin (+) and short thick (−) lines!

2. Hands-On Physical Kit Bridge

Run Station 4 first so pupils predict what happens when opening the switch or flipping cell 2. Then hand out trays for Worksheet 1 Page 2: Circuit A (1 cell + closed switch + lamp) and Circuit B (2 cells + open switch + buzzer + lamp).

3. Red-Flag Misconception Watch

Watch for pupils drawing the horizontal wire straight across the cell symbol, or confusing the Lamp (× inside circle) with the Buzzer (semicircle D-cup with 2 parallel legs). Use David's Board (W) to model erasing slots first.
