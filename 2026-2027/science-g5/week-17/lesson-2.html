🌍 Real-World Science: Why Homes Use Parallel Circuits

🏫
Classroom Ceiling Lights

Wired in parallel! Every light tube gets full voltage, stays equally bright, and if one tube blows, the rest stay ON.

🚗
Car Headlights Safety

Wired in parallel so if the left headlight breaks at night, the right headlight stays brightly lit and keeps the driver safe!

🎄
Old Series Fairy Lights

Cheap old fairy lights were wired in series. When just ONE tiny bulb broke, the single loop opened and the ENTIRE string went dark!

🚨 Misconception Buster!

Myth: "Lamp 1 in a series circuit is brighter because it gets the electricity first!" — FALSE! In a series circuit, BOTH identical lamps share the 3.0 V equally (1.5 V each) and have the EXACT SAME dimmer brightness!

📋 Steps to Success

  1. 1Trace from + to − with your finger. Count the loops!
  2. 21 loop = Series (voltage is shared ➔ lamps dimmer, equal).
  3. 32+ branches = Parallel (each branch gets full voltage ➔ bright!).
  4. 4Break test: If 1 loop breaks, Series stops; Parallel branches keep flowing!
⚡ Kit Safety Check

• Use 1.5 V cells ONLY (never mains sockets).

• Open switch before moving crocodile clips.

• Check cell direction: + to − in batteries.

Red-Flag Hinge Check (Minute 35)

Ask: "In a 2-lamp series circuit, is Lamp 1 brighter than Lamp 2?" If >20% say YES, pause immediately and demonstrate physically with 2 identical bulbs on 3.0 V — swap the bulbs' positions to prove both glow equally dimly!

Hands-On Kit Pacing Tip

Have groups build Series 1 ➔ 2 ➔ 3 first (takes 8 mins), record observations, then convert to Parallel 2 ➔ 3 by clipping the 2nd lamp's leads directly across Lamp 1's terminals (ladder shape). Then unscrew Lamp 1!

EAL Sentence Stems
  • "In a series circuit, lamps are dimmer because they share the voltage."
  • "In a parallel circuit, each branch receives the full voltage."
  • "When one lamp breaks in parallel, the other lamps stay ON because their loops are still complete."
