🌍 Real-World Science & Engineering Relevance

Where Do Engineers Use Unit 5 Forces Every Day?

🚀
Lunar Rover Engineers

Rovers weigh only 1/6 as much on the Moon, so NASA designs lightweight wheels and special springs for lower gravity while keeping the exact same mass of cameras and batteries.

🚢
Hai Phong Cargo Ship Designers

Massive steel ships carry thousands of tonnes of goods across Vietnam’s ports because their wide, hollow hulls displace enormous volumes of seawater, creating huge upthrust.

🪂
Parachute & Aeroplane Safety

Parachutes open wide to catch air resistance until upward air resistance balances downward weight, allowing a safe, steady landing speed instead of dangerous acceleration.

Busting the Top 3 Cambridge Forces Exam Traps

Exam Trap #1 · 6Pf.01 & 6Pf.02
🛡️

Checkpoint Survival Checklist

  1. 1 Mass = kg = NEVER changes in space. Weight = N = changes with gravity.
  2. 2 Earth Shortcut: Weight (N) ≈ Mass (kg) × 10. Moon: Divide Earth weight by 6!
  3. 3 Arrow Rules: Arrows MUST touch the object, use a ruler, point accurately, and carry a clear force name.
  4. 4 Floating = Balanced! Draw Upthrust arrow the exact same length as Weight arrow.
  5. 5 Shape Magic: Wide hollow shape ➔ displaces more water ➔ bigger upthrust ➔ floats!

Mark 1 (Pattern): As the boat’s rim height increases, the maximum number of marbles it holds increases (ignoring the anomalous result at 4 cm, 8 marbles).

Mark 2 (Scientific Reason): A taller hollow rim can push aside (displace) a greater volume of water before sinking, which produces a larger upward upthrust force.

SC 3 · Shape & Upthrust NOT YET MET

A ball of modelling clay sinks, yet the exact same clay pressed into a wide hollow bowl floats.

Write one scientific sentence using the words displaced water and upthrust.

The wide hollow bowl pushes aside (displaces) more water, which increases the upthrust until it equals the clay’s weight.

Red-Flag Hinge Check

Watch for pupils saying “mass gets smaller on the Moon”, drawing floating upthrust arrows shorter than weight arrows, or confusing kg with N.

Teacher Support Table Intervention

Pull stuck learners to the support table with two cards: MASS = stuff inside me = kg = NEVER changes vs WEIGHT = gravity’s pull = N = changes on Moon. Check equal floating arrows with a centimetre ruler.

Whole-Class Backup Pivot (>30% Stuck)

Pause for a 3-minute Human Force-Arrow Freeze: pupils stand and use both arms to show equal balanced arrows (floating raft) vs one long arm + one short arm (sinking iron anchor).
