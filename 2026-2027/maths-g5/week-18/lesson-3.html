🌍 Why Does This Matter in Real Life?

Vietnam Railways & Precision Transport Scheduling

🚆

Reunification Express (SE1–SE8)

Railway computers calculate speeds in decimal hours (like 2.4 h between Vinh and Dong Hoi), then convert into exact 24-hour station departure boards for passengers.

✈️

Noi Bai & Da Nang Connections

If a shuttle timetable says 0.4 hours and a traveller guesses 40 minutes instead of 24 minutes, they misplan their transfer by 16 minutes and risk missing their gate!

🚇

Hanoi Metro Line 2A Headways

Urban metro trains run on tight decimal-minute intervals (such as 6.5 min = 6 min 30 s) so platforms stay safe and punctual during rush hour.

🚨 If They Aren't Getting It (Live Intervention)

Red-Flag Hinge: Pupils add decimal hours directly onto clock times (08:30 + 1.4 h = 09:70 or 09:44) instead of multiplying 0.4 × 60 = 24 min first.

Support Table Rule Card: "Step 1: Convert EVERY decimal into h & min FIRST. Step 2: Add minutes carefully — regroup every 60 minutes into 1 hour!"

Whole-Class Pivot (>30% stuck): Freeze 2 mins on David's Board (`W`), jump along an open timeline: 09:45 + 1 h 24 min (09:45 ➔ 10:45 ➔ 11:00 ➔ 11:09).
