Why Does This Matter in Real Life?

🏊

Olympic Swimming & Sprints

Pool touchpads and athletics stopwatches record race splits in seconds and decimals. Coaches convert between 2.4 min and 2 min 24 s to see who won by a few seconds!

🏃

Hanoi School Sports Day

When one referee reads a digital timer in decimal minutes and another reads minutes and seconds, converting both ways keeps the medal leaderboard fair.

🚌

Transport & Fitness Apps

Hanoi bus schedules, flight trackers and cycling apps calculate average speed using decimal hours (2 h 15 min = 2.25 h, never 2.15 h!).

I Do • Visual Stage

➔ FORWARD (Decimal ➔ Mixed): MULTIPLY BY 60

1. Keep the whole number untouched. 2. Multiply ONLY the decimal part by 60. 3. Write both units cleanly.

⬅ BACKWARD (Mixed ➔ Decimal): DIVIDE BY 60

1. Keep the bigger unit as the whole number. 2. Put the smaller unit over 60 (÷ 60). 3. Simplify to tenths or quarters!

🧠 Reasoning & Justification Lab (TWM)

Part A: Decide True or False and prove it with a calculation. Part B: Spot the reverse-conversion mistake!

Part B • Spot the Reverse Mistake Convincing & Critiquing

Who is right: Nam or Mai?

💬 Nam says:

"To convert 3 min 30 s into decimal minutes, I just put a decimal point between them: 3.30 min."

💬 Mai says:

"No! You must divide 30 seconds by 60 first: 30 ÷ 60 = 0.5, so 3 min 30 s = 3.5 min."

Mai is correct! Nam used base-100 thinking (30/100 = 0.30). Because 1 minute = 60 seconds, 30 seconds is half a minute: 30 ÷ 60 = 1/2 = 0.5. Therefore 3 min 30 s = 3.5 min (whereas 3.30 min = 3 min 18 s!).
Always, Sometimes, or Never?

"Converting a minutes and b seconds into decimal minutes gives a.b minutes."

SOMETIMES TRUE! It ONLY works when b = 0 (e.g. 4 min 0 s = 4.0 min). For every non-zero number of seconds (1 to 59), dividing by 60 gives a different decimal from 0.b!

• "This is false because there are 60 seconds/minutes, not 100."

• "To convert forward, I multiply the decimal part by 60: ___ × 60 = ___."

• "To convert back, I divide the smaller unit by 60: ___ ÷ 60 = ___."
