Unit 7.1 · Rectangles and Triangles

Estimating & Calculating Right-Angled Triangle Areas

In Lesson 1, your child discovers that every right-angled triangle is an exact half of a rectangle: Area = (base × height) ÷ 2 — and learns how to avoid multiplying by the sloping side!

🍽️ 3-Tier Dinner Table Talks

Quick 2-minute family prompts using everyday objects around the house!

🟢 Tier 1 · Paper Napkin Fold

Fold a rectangular napkin or A4 sheet diagonally corner-to-corner. Ask: 'If the whole sheet is 600 cm², what is the area of ONE folded triangle?'

🟠 Tier 2 · Tiling a Room

Look at a rectangular floor tile or tabletop. Ask: 'How many right-angled triangular tiles of area 15 cm² would fit inside a 30 cm² rectangle?'

🟣 Tier 3 · Boat Sails Challenge

A boat sail is a right-angled triangle with perpendicular sides 4 m and 6 m, and a sloping edge of 7.2 m. Ask: 'Why must the sailmaker ignore 7.2 m when finding area?'
