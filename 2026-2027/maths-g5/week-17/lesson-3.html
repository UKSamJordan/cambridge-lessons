Red-Flag Misconception Watch

1. Forgetting ÷ 2: Area Accountants often multiply base × height for the Koi Pond or Flowerbed and forget to halve it, blowing the 600-credit budget.
2. Non-90° Slant Sides: Remind Chief Architects that BOTH straight sides of every triangle must lie along horizontal & vertical grid lines!
3. Leftover Gaps: Show how pairing two right-angled triangles inside one bounding rectangle guarantees zero awkward gaps.

EAL Speaking Stems for Pitch

  • "Our right-angled triangle has a base of ___ m and a perpendicular height of ___ m."
  • "We halved the rectangle area so the triangle equals ___ m²."
  • "To stay under 600 credits, we swapped ___ m² of ___ for ___."
