Live Intervention & Misconception Watch

  • Pit-Stop 1 Red Flag (40 m² or 75.2 m²): If >20% forget ÷ 2 or multiply by the 9.4 m sloping side, open David's Board (W) and draw the enclosing 8 × 5 rectangle split diagonally!
  • Pit-Stop 2 Red Flag (forgetting difference): Pupils often subtract 6 × 8 = 48 instead of 6 × (8 − 3) = 30. Re-show the Swap Simulator slider at 6 m².
  • Team Bottleneck Fix: If Role 4 is idle during Cycle 1, assign them to pre-label all zone titles and dimensions in pencil directly on Page 2.

EAL Speaking Stems for Pitch

  • "We halved the rectangle area so the triangle equals ___ m²."
