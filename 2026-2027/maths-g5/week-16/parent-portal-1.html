What Your Child Learned Today

In Lesson 1, pupils revised how to calculate mode (including bimodal data), median, mean and range, choose the fairest average when an extreme value appears, label circle parts (centre, radius, diameter, circumference), classify quadrilaterals by their diagonals, and find rotational symmetry up to Order 4.

1. Key Concepts at a Glance (Watch Out for Common Traps!)

Averages Checklist
  • Mode = most frequent value (a set can have two modes — bimodal — or no mode).
  • Always ORDER numbers smallest to largest before finding the Median.
  • Mean = Total sum ÷ How many values (0 still counts as a value!).
  • Range = Largest − Smallest (measures spread, not an average).
Circles & Rotational Symmetry
  • Diameter = 2 × Radius. Always set a compass to the RADIUS!
  • Square = Order 4 | Rectangle, Rhombus, Parallelogram = Order 2.
  • Trap Alert: Minimum rotational symmetry is ALWAYS Order 1 (never Order 0!).

3. Dinner Table Talks — 5-Minute Family Chats

Tier 1 • Starter (Everyday Circles)

Look at a dinner plate or bowl on the table. Ask your child: "If this plate is 22 cm wide across the centre, what is its radius? And what do we call the rim all the way around the outside?"

Radius = 22 ÷ 2 = 11 cm. The distance all the way around the outside edge is the circumference.
Tier 2 • Core (Rotational Symmetry Hunt)

Point to a rectangular dining table or smartphone screen and a ceiling fan with 3 blades. Ask: "If we spin each one a full 360° turn, how many times does it match its starting shape? What order of rotational symmetry is that?"

A rectangle matches twice (at 180° and 360°) ➔ Order 2. A symmetrical 3-blade ceiling fan matches 3 times (every 120°) ➔ Order 3!
Tier 3 • Stretch (Smart Hanoi Shopper)

Five bowls of phở on a street cost (thousand VND): 40, 45, 45, 50, and 120 (a luxury wagyu bowl). Ask your child to find the Mean (60) and Median (45) and explain which average tells a tourist the fairest typical price.

Total = 300 ➔ Mean = 300 ÷ 5 = 60. Median = 45. The Median (45) is much fairer because the single extreme price (120) pulls the mean higher than four of the five shops!

4. Quick 3-Minute Home Check

Q1: Find the median of 15, 7, 21, 9, 13.

Order first: 7, 9, 13, 15, 21 ➔ Median = 13.

Q2: To draw a circle with a 28 cm diameter, what width should you set your compass to?

14 cm (always set the compass to the radius = 28 ÷ 2).

Q3: True or False — A kite has rotational symmetry of Order 0.

FALSE! Every shape matches itself once after a full 360° turn, so a kite has Order 1.
