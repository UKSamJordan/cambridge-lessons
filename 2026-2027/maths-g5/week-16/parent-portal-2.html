🟢 Tier 1 (Must) • Metric Units & Prices

Our rice bag weighs 2.35 kg. How many grams is that (× 1000), and what is 2.35 kg rounded to the nearest tenth?

2.35 × 1000 = 2350 g (every digit shifts 3 columns LEFT!). Rounded to the nearest tenth: 2.4 kg because the hundredths digit 5 rounds 3 up to 4.
🟠 Tier 2 (Should) • Lifts & Mountain Weather

We park in basement B2 (−2) and ride the lift up 11 floors. Which floor do we reach? And if Fansipan is −3°C at night and +8°C at midday, how many degrees did it warm up?

Always ask: 'How many steps to reach 0 first?' From −2 to 0 is +2, then +9 more reaches Floor 9! For Fansipan: −3°C to 0°C is +3°C, plus +8°C = 11°C rise.
🟣 Tier 3 (Could) • Pattern Spotting at Home

Each dining table seats 4 people on the sides plus 2 people at the two ends. If we push 10 tables in a long row (4n + 2), how many guests can sit down?

Substitute n = 10 into 4n + 2 ➔ 4 × 10 + 2 = 42 guests! Follow-up challenge: How many tables would we need for 30 guests? (4n + 2 = 30 ➔ 4n = 28 ➔ 7 tables).
