⚖️ Who Is Correct?

Improper Fraction Operator

Mai says: "5/4 of 20 must be smaller than 20 because it is a fraction."

Khoa says: "5/4 of 20 is 25 because 5/4 is greater than 1 whole."

Khoa is correct! 5/4 is an improper fraction (1 whole and 1/4). 20 ÷ 4 = 5, then 5 × 5 = 25, which is greater than 20.
🔍 Always, Sometimes, or Never?

Bigger Denominator Rule

"A fraction with a bigger denominator is always a smaller fraction."

SOMETIMES TRUE! True when numerators are equal (1/8 < 1/4), but FALSE when numerators differ (7/8 > 1/4). Always check BOTH numerator and denominator!
💬 Convince Me!

Prove the Order Two Ways

Convince your partner that 3/4 > 70% > 0.65 using percentages AND decimals.

Method 1 (Percentages): 3/4 = 75%, 70%, 0.65 = 65% → 75% > 70% > 65%.
Method 2 (Decimals): 3/4 = 0.75, 70% = 0.70, 0.65 → 0.75 > 0.70 > 0.65.
