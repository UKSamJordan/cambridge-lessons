Steps to Success — Averages

  1. Order the data from smallest to largest first.
  2. Mode: spot the most frequent value (can be bimodal).
  3. Median: cross off outer pairs to find the middle value.
  4. Range: subtract smallest from largest (Max − Min).
  5. Mean: add all values, then divide by how many values.

Steps to Success — 2D Shapes

  1. Circle rule: Diameter = 2 × Radius (always set compass to radius!).
  2. Diagonals: check if equal, if they bisect, and if they cross at 90°.
  3. Rotational symmetry: spin a full 360° and count matches (minimum is always Order 1).
