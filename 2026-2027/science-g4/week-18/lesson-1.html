🔬 Scientific Mechanism Explainer: Why Do Teeth & Saliva Work Together?

Discuss with your partner first, then click to reveal the science!

1. Teeth Break Food Down

Chewing cuts and grinds large solid bites into much smaller pieces so they are safe to swallow and easier to digest.

2. Saliva Softens & Starts Digestion

Saliva wets dry food so it slides easily down the oesophagus AND starts breaking starch in bread or crackers into slightly sweeter sugars!

3. Ready for Absorption Later

Only tiny dissolved nutrients can pass through the wall of the small intestine into the blood — chewing starts that vital job!

🚨 #1 Science Misconception Alert

Myth: "The stomach absorbs all our nutrients!"

❌ FALSE!

The stomach churns food and mixes it with acid to break it into a thick liquid — it does NOT absorb nutrients into the blood!

✅ Small Intestine

Absorbs tiny dissolved nutrients into the blood! (Small = Super nutrients)

✅ Large Intestine

Absorbs water back into the body so solid waste forms! (Large = Liquid water)
